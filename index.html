How it works

Built around speed, visible uncertainty and human control

Six steps from the final whistle to a decision your staff can act on and audit.

1

Accept the match

Upload the video and confirm the line-up. We check the footage is suitable for the outputs you need.

2

Track players and ball

The pitch is calibrated, players and the ball are tracked, and events and tactical patterns are built on top.

3

Surface uncertainty

Low-confidence identities, occlusions and ball gaps are flagged instead of hidden.

4

Verify quickly

Your analyst reviews the sections that matter, fixes identities or events, and keeps control of the football interpretation.

5

Deliver decisions

Coaches receive a short brief, linked clips and a searchable match, without rebuilding the analysis.

6

Learn from correction

Accepted corrections improve your club profile and the workflow over time, subject to explicit data rights.

A coach can act on it. An analyst can audit it. A player can understand it.

What we build

A focused performance product, not a wall of metrics

Every conclusion links back to the source video. Every output answers a question a coach or analyst actually asks.

Match Intelligence

What happened, where did the structure break, and what deserves review?

A searchable match timeline with linked events, team shape, line height, pressing, transitions, progression, set pieces and coach playlists.

Opposition Intelligence

How does the opponent build, press, transition and defend?

Pre-match dossiers with exploitable patterns, expected structure, build-up and pressing maps, set-piece notes and supporting clips.

Player Development

How is the player changing, and which behaviours should be coached next?

Season profiles, role-based metrics, off-ball contribution, technical actions, trends, clips and coach comments.

Analyst Review Studio

Where is the system uncertain, and how can the analyst correct it fast?

Confidence queues, identity correction, event review, clip validation and version history. It makes the analyst faster, not invisible.
